How it works

From domain to report in four steps — most of it automatic.

01

Add a domain

Enter the domain or subdomain you want tested. One domain costs one credit.

02

Verify by TXT

We auto-generate a unique TXT record. Copy it into your DNS provider and hit verify.

03

We pentest

The engine scans your surface — headers, injection, auth, misconfig, secrets, and more.

04

Get the report

A prioritized, human-reviewed report with severity, proof, and fix guidance — fast.
